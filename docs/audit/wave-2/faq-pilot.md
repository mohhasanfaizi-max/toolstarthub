## Sample and method

Selected all seven CSV rows with `Priority=High` from `/workspace/tsh/audit-2026-11-07/english-content-quality.csv`, plus loan-calculator, character-counter and image-compressor. Total **10 English pages: five tools, five guides; 146 rendered FAQs**. Selection metadata is saved in evidence/faq-selected-pages.json. The seven High rows were unambiguous.

Extracted actual FAQPage JSON-LD from local production HTTP 200 pages, saved the HTML and compared to implementation. FAQ numbering below is the rendered order (not source-array indices). Evidence: faq-rendered-extracts.json, faq-extracts.txt, faq-similarity.csv and faq-behavior-tests.txt.

Similarity was computed for **all 1,145 within-page pairs**. Lowercase alphanumeric token sets drop a documented short list of common function words. **J** is Jaccard similarity of combined question+answer tokens: intersection/union. **S** is Python difflib.SequenceMatcher answer-text ratio with autojunk disabled. Question-only Jaccard is also included in the CSV. Scores are triage evidence, not semantic truth. Flag S≥0.65 or combined J≥0.50 for review, then manually inspect identical intent even below thresholds. A high lexical score can be a false positive (humanizer “free” versus “how it works” share tool-name words); a low score can miss paraphrased duplicates. No similarity threshold automatically justifies deletion.

“Unsupported” means no claim-specific primary support or individual clinical validation was established in this pilot, not a declaration that the claim is false. Code verifies implementation, not health outcomes. NEEDS OWNER marks reviewer/provider/device evidence unavailable here.

## Recommended rules for a wider pass

1. **Task usefulness before counts.** No FAQ minimum, fixed quota or automatic deletion target. Six accurate questions may be better than twenty generic ones.
2. **Verify final assembled pages.** Review original FAQs plus appended banks together, both visible UI and FAQPage schema; avoid assessing an unused source array.
3. **One distinct user intent per answer.** Use Jaccard/sequence flags plus manual semantic review. Consolidate only when the retained answer preserves unique instructions, examples and caveats. Keep distinct calculation examples even when vocabulary overlaps.
4. **Bind each capability statement to code and a test.** Exact input limits, units, supported formats, fee handling, output availability and local/remote paths must match the current implementation. Clearly label general education as outside supported functionality.
5. **Treat modes separately.** Deterministic local rewriting is not Gemini generation; gram portions are not ml without density; a backend helper is not proof of a UI feature.
6. **YMYL evidence matrix.** Tie nutritional/financial outcome claims to a dated primary source and actual scope; have a real qualified reviewer approve clinical advice. Never invent credentials. No implied guaranteed loss pace, safety floor, lender quote or detector outcome.
7. **Privacy and provider claims need actual data flow/account evidence.** Avoid unsupported retention assurances. Recheck each route and saved-data field when features change.
8. **Preserve accurate specifics.** Keep worked numbers and meaningful caveats; do not rewrite to sound “less AI” or remove useful FAQs based on style, word count, or detector scores.
9. **Small approved batches.** Present exact before/after proposals, affected URLs, risk and test cases; make no FAQ edits until approved. Retest both visible FAQ and structured-data parity; maintain URLs/titles/robots/sitemap.
10. **Negative controls and uncertainty.** Include already-good pages; explicitly record no defect where appropriate. Mark missing clinical, provider, or real-device evidence NEEDS OWNER; do not extrapolate traffic, ranking or approval outcomes.

## Differences from the original audit / wave 1

- All seven High-priority pages were identifiable, but “FAQ padding” is not a universal 20-question pattern: four selected guides have 10 questions and image-compressor has six. Several are distinct and useful.
- Loan guide’s majority-interest contradiction and the two FDA bibliography links were already corrected by wave 1. They are not reported as still broken.
- Character counter uses Unicode code points, not user-perceived emoji/graphemes; the generic FAQ bank obscures that distinction.
- Alma custom-food micronutrient claims are not matched by the current input fields. Wave-2 packaged ml handling makes the existing broad “millilitres” FAQ require a narrowly scoped follow-up, but this read-only pilot leaves it unchanged.
- No claim of AI authorship, “AI-detection failure,” SEO penalty, traffic damage or AdSense outcome is made.

No FAQ content, page titles, URLs, routes, robots or sitemap was edited for item 2. No browser E2E, real-device compression test, or live Gemini call ran.
